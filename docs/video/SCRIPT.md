# Firebreak: 30-second demo video script

Status: draft 1. Locked choices: hybrid film (animated open and end card, real captures of the live site between them), narration on, 16:9.
Voice: a generated voice as the timing track first; a teammate may record over it. Open: required length and format per the submission rules (not read yet).

## The one idea

A NASA researcher must leave with this: Firebreak takes NASA's scattered fire experiments and answers, for one habitat, what matters, what it means, what a fire would do, and how sure we are.

## Story, shot by shot

The middle of the film follows the guided path on the site (docs/RESKIN_PRD.md section 16), one step per shot. Each capture opens from a URL (`?guide=N&base=moon`), so a shot can be re-recorded after a site change.

## Claims and where each is backed

- "Never in Moon gravity": the site states that the PSI data has no partial-gravity (Moon or Mars) data. Say "tests in this data" on screen if a judge could read it as a claim about all of NASA. [confirm wording with the team]
- "Stop the ventilation ... what the records say": BASS-II fabric tests and ACME burner results, as cited on the outcome card. Use the cloth start point in orbit air or the base where the card reads "The flame goes out". [pick the base after checking the card on Moon Base]
- "Every number comes from the data": the "What the data shows" lines are computed from the tables, not written by the model.
- No Artemis date anywhere unless a source is supplied.

## Rules

- No faces, no full names of minors. Voice only.
- Captions burned in for every narration line, so the film works with sound off.
- Pacing: no shot under 3.5 seconds, one idea per shot, cuts land on the first word of each line.
